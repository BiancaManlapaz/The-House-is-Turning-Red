In the story, the house becomes red through the process of the man (who I'm sure you've identified as the colonizer) erasing Indigenous identity and effectively killing the inhabitants of the house because settler colonialism requires the genocide of the Indigenous.

The timeline roughly follows the years 1493 to 1850, although settler colonialism persists after those years and until today. It is important to note that the transformation of the house is not strictly applicable to the given years. Settler colonalism, and the turning of the house is a process that is consistently repeated across time and space. All houses can eventually become red.
